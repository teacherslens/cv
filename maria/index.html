Profile

I am an international educator and Learning Support Specialist with 20 years in international schools in Colombia, Sudan and Panama. I support neurodiverse learners through case management, IEP and Accommodation Plan development, MTSS, UDL and data-informed intervention.

My work runs from the individual student to the whole school: I have helped design and refine intervention systems and student support processes, led and supported professional learning in literacy and mathematics, and taken on teacher leadership, team facilitation, hiring and schoolwide initiatives.

I work in close partnership with teachers, specialists, families and support teams, with a student-centered approach focused on strengthening inclusive practice and building educator capacity.
